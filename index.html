Privacy policy

Last updated 31 October 2026

This website doesn't collect your personal data. Your order goes from your phone to the shop on WhatsApp, and nothing is sent until you press send there.

What the website stores

To make ordering easier, the site saves three things in your own browser, on your own device:

  • the items in your order, so they're still there if you reload the page
  • your name, so you don't have to type it again next time
  • the time of your last order, to prevent accidental repeat orders

This information stays on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. You can remove it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

When you place an order

Tapping "Send order on WhatsApp" opens WhatsApp with a ready-made message that you can read and change. If you send it, the shop receives:

  • your name, the items you ordered, and whether it's for pickup or delivery
  • your delivery address and any notes, if you added them
  • your WhatsApp number and profile, as with any WhatsApp message

We use this only to prepare your order, deliver it, and contact you about it. We don't sell it, share it with anyone else, or use it for marketing.

No tracking

This website has no cookies, analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind, and you don't need an account to use it.

Other services

The site links to WhatsApp, Google Maps and Instagram. These are run by other companies, and their own privacy policies apply once you open them.
